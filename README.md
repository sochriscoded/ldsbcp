# ldsbcp
I'm Anglican and Exmormon. This is my imagination of what would have happened if Joseph Smith had been Anglican before forming the LDS church. More than anything, it is to create, develop, and serve as a guide for prayer, give holidays and a liturgy/liturgical calendar, and provide opprotunity for further spiritual growth for those who are LDS.

# Introduction

I grew up LDS but always took interest in other faiths. This would culminate in me traveling to other churches and taking classes on historical theology and comparative religion during my undergrad at BYU. Two particular faiths I was most investe in were Catholosism and the Anglican Communion. Their structured mass settings, coupled with strong daily prayer cycles and liturgical calendars drew me into them upon dealing with a faith crisis at the end of my time at BYU. It was in the Episcopal Church that I found peace and solace while I worked out for myself "With fear and trembling" my own path, purpose and meaning in life thanks in large part to the book of common prayer.
