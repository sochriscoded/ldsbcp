# Common Prayers, Statements, and Responses

## The Lord's Prayer

Our Father who art in Heaven
Hallowed be Thy Name
Thy Kingdom Come,thy Will be done
On Earth as it is in Heaven

Give us this day our daily bread
And forgive us our trespasses as we forgive those who tresspass against us
And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil
For thine is the kingdom, and the power, and the glory, forever and ever
Amen.

## Glory Be

Glory Be to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit:
As it was in the beginning, is now, and ever shall be, forever. Amen.

## Glory Be (Alternative)

Glory Be to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit:
As it was in the beginning, is now, and ever shall be, worlds without end. Amen.

## Blessed Be God

Blessed be God the Father, and His Son, and the Holy Spirit
*And blessed be God's Kingdom now and forever, amen.*

## Bless the Lord

Bless the Lord who forgives our sins
*His Mercy endures forever*

## Blessed Be God 2

Blessed be God
*For ever and ever, amen.*

## Christ is Risen

**Alleluia** Christ is risen
*He is risen indeed!* **Alleluia**

## At The Opening of Morning or Evening Prayer

Lord, open thou our lips
*and our mouths shall show forth thy praise*

(*OR*)

Lord, open our lips
*and our mouth shall proclaim thy praise*

(*OR*)

Lord, make speed to save us
*Oh Lord, make haste to help us*

## Our Help

Our help is in the name of the Lord
*The Maker of Heaven and Earth*
Blessed be the Name of the Lord
*From this time forward and forever more*

## O Savior of the World

O Savior of the World, who by his Atonement has redeemed us,
*Save us and help us, we humbly beseech thee*

## We Adore Thee

We adore thee, o Redeemer of Israel, and we bless thee,
*because by thy Atonement thou has redeemed the world*

## Rest Eternal

Rest Eternal grant (him/her/them) o Lord,
*and let Light Eternal, shine upon (him/her/them)*
may (his/her/their) soul(s) and the souls of all those departed rest in peace, through the mercy of God. Amen.

## Take this Cup

Take this Cup from us o Lord
*Not my will, but thine be done*

Into Thy hands I commend my spirit
*Remember me when you come into your kingdom*

(*Silence*)

We adore you, O Lord
*For by your Atonement, we may gain Eternal Life with thee*

## Doxology

Praise God from Whom All Blessings Flow, Praise ye him all creatures here below; Praise him above, ye heavenly hosts
Priase Father, Son, and Holy Ghost.
