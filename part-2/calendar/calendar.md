# The Calendar

NOTE: Easter, Lent and Advent move throughout the year, thus they are not included in this calendar. These are fixed, unmoved dates. If Easter falls on the same days as the ones listed below, or if  holy week occurs at the same time as the April Restoration feasts, they are concelebrated as they are both considered equally important.

Likewise, General Conference happens twice a year on a Saturday and Sunday. These will be considered Major Significant Feast Days, on par with the other Major, Significant Feast Days.

## The Type of Feasts

**FEASTS OF JESUS AND HIS LIFE (Holy Days)**
**_FEASTS OF JESUS IN THE RESTORATION (Holy Days)_**
**_Major feasts in the restoration (major)_**
**Major feasts, not essential but significant.**

Minor or lesser feasts

**A note about feasts:**

Not every feast is given on its exact historical day. For example: Winter Quarters day and reorganization day may not have happened exactly on January 3rd and 4th, but they happened around this time, and due to there be preexisting feasts on these days, impact when they should be appropriately celebrated.
