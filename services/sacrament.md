## The Opening Hymn

*(The service opens either by one who is presiding, or the presiding authority may choose someone else to conduct the service as the celebrant:)*

An Opening Hymn or Psalm is sung, chanted, or recited. If the occation permits or is deemed appropriate, there may be a procession. Upon the completion of the hymn, the provider or the one who has been delegated the authority, the celebrant may say the following:

**Celebrant:** Blessed be God the Father, His Son Jesus Christ, and the Holy Spirit.
**Ppeople:**and blessed be God’s kingdom now and forever.
**All:** **Amen**

(*The celebrant or their delegated individual will offer a prayer, either from the heart or the following may be used:*)

## A Collect for Purity

  Heavenly Father, unto whom all hearts are open, all desires known, and from whom no secrets are hid: Cleanse the thoughts of our hearts by the inspiration of thy Holy Spirit, that we may perfectly love thee, and worthily magnify thy holy Name; through Jesus Christ our Lord Amen.

The celbrant will then say:

**C:** Glory Be to the Father, to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, 
**All:** Amen.

## The Summary of the Law

*A reader will then say the following:*

The Summary of the Law, given by our Lord Jesus Christ in Matthew 22:

  Thou shalt love the Lord thy God with all thy heart, and with all thy soul, and with all thy mind. This is the first and great commandment. And the second is like unto it: Thou shalt love thy neighbor as thyself. On these two commandments hang all the Law and the Prophets.

(*The reader will then say:*)

Dear Brother and Sisters, let us remember these words as we renew our covenants before God, his angels, and this congregation as our witnesses.

(*If the season permits, other saying and verses may be used on occation and can be found elsewhere in this book*)

# The Liturgy of the Sacrament

### The Profession of Faith

*The Celebrant then says*

O: Let us Profess our Faith Before God and This Congregation as Witnesses

R:

We believe in God, the Eternal Father,
and in His Only Begotten Son, Jesus Christ, Our Lord.
He came down from heaven,
For us men and for our salvation
and by the Holy Spirit was born of the Virgin Mary,
and became man.

For our sake he was crucified under Pontius Pilate,
he suffered death and was buried,
and rose again on the third day
in accordance with the Scriptures.
He ascended into heaven
and is seated at the right hand of the Father.

He will come again in glory
to judge the living and the dead
and his kingdom will have no end.

We believe in the Holy Spirit, The Comforter, the giver of life,
We believe salvation through the Atonement of Jesus Christ,
One Holy, True, and Apostolic Church,
One Baptism for the remission of sins,
and obedience to the commandments and ordinances of God.
We look for the resurrection of the dead,
and Eternal Life and Exhaltation in the world to come. Amen.

### The Confession of Sin

*The Bishop or their authorized representative says the following*

Let us humbly confess our sins unto Almighty God.

*(Silence may be kept.)*

*the bishop/celebrant and the people together say the following:*

Almighty God, Our Father in Heaven,
Father of our Lord Jesus Christ,
We acknowledge that we have sinned against thee,
by thought, word, and deed,
by what we have done,
and what we have not done.
We have not loved thee with our whole hearts;
We have not loved our neighbors as ourselves,
and we have failed to uphold our covenants and promises to thee.
We are truly sorry and we humbly repent.
Have mercy upon us and grant by taking this sacrament we may renew our promises to thee,
and find by the mercy of thy son, Jesus Christ, eternal light and life,
that we may delight in thy will,
and walk in thy ways
to the honor and glory of thy Name;
through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.
